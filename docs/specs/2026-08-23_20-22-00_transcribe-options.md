**What I would use**

* **You want a free app and do not want to code:** use **HebrewScribe**. It is currently the most sensible starting point: local, unlimited, no account, batch-oriented, and it uses Ivrit-AI’s Hebrew model. Your audio stays on your device.
* **You can use a good NVIDIA GPU and want best Hebrew text quality:** test **Caspi-1.7B** against Ivrit-AI. Caspi’s own evaluation claims lower WER for conversational/WhatsApp-style Hebrew, while Ivrit did slightly better on KAN broadcast audio. That is promising, not independent proof. [Caspi evaluation details](https://huggingface.co/OzLabs/Caspi-1.7B)
* **Your recordings genuinely alternate Hebrew and English within sentences:** **VibeVoice** is the most interesting free candidate because it is explicitly designed for code-switching, speaker attribution, and hour-long files. But I would not make it your only archive pipeline until it wins a comparison against Ivrit/Caspi on one of your recordings. [Microsoft’s own caveat is here](https://github.com/microsoft/VibeVoice).

**About the VibeVoice entries**

Use only Microsoft’s official `microsoft/VibeVoice-ASR` or the Transformers-friendly `microsoft/VibeVoice-ASR-HF`. The `bezzam`, `Sherckuith`, `Creador301`, `Matir`, `Jinstudio`, and ONNX entries are forks/packaging variants. They may make installation easier, but they are not independent evidence that one transcription model is better.

**Other usable apps, but not separate ASR breakthroughs**

* [Ivrit Transcriber](https://github.com/ilankt/ivrit-transcriber): local desktop app with SRT/TXT, VAD, and GPU support. It uses the Ivrit/Whisper path.
* [hebrew-audio-transcriber](https://github.com/KoganTheDev/hebrew-audio-transcriber): another faster-whisper GUI. Also essentially a packaging choice around Whisper, not a new Hebrew model.

There is no credible independent, apples-to-apples public benchmark yet for **your exact Hebrew-English meeting audio** across Ivrit, Caspi, VibeVoice, and the cloud vendors. The honest way to avoid trying ten websites is to compare only these three local model families on one representative recording, then use the winner for all files.
